About Me!

Hi. My name is Mikaela Villarin. Most people call me Ella, Miks, or Kay, but it really just depends on you. I don’t really care what you call me unless it’s something stupid. I’m 14 years old, and I was born on August 28, 2011. I don’t remember exactly where I was born, but I did grow up in Consolacion, Cebu. There’s nothing really interesting about my past—well, nothing interesting you should know. The most interesting things about my past are also my deepest, darkest secrets. However, if you’re close to me, I might be able to tell you what.


Anyway, I’m a ninth grader in high school. I go to the Philippine Science High School—not the main campus, though! I’m not smart enough for that. My campus is the Central Visayas campus, or as most people call it, CVisC. My favourite subjects are currently physics, computer science, chemistry, and social science. One of my most favourite subjects used to be math, but you can guess that it went all downhill. What happened? Honestly, I’m asking myself the same thing. I’m kind of disappointed in myself, really. I used to be a top student back in elementary, but I guess after surrounding myself with plenty of top students now, I kind of lost my motivation to keep going for a place on the podium. Okay, enough about that. My least favourite subject is Filipino, and everyone knows that. I don’t know, okay? My first language was English, and I think I’m pretty good at it.


I come from a small but lively bunch I call my family. I’m the eldest daughter of three siblings. My little sister, Riki Gabrielle, is 12 years old. She trains almost every weekend to be a model, and she participates in lots of modeling competitions. My little brother, Lucas, is 5 years old. He is basically the ray of sunshine that keeps our family together. My mother, Jonalyn, works as a freelancer and an owner of multiple businesses. We’ve had plenty of businesses before, like a baking business, a make-up one, a party-organizing one, a computer-related one, and lots more. She’s the reason we eat healthily and travel to a lot of places. My family and I have been to Hong Kong, Thailand, Taiwan, and more! My personal favourite is probably Thailand, considering how many times I’ve been there. Anyway, my father, Joel, loves sports. He mainly plays basketball, but he also works as part of an organization called The Eagles, where they help people around Cebu. My family and I have had plenty of fallouts and bad times, but I would not trade them for anything.


Lastly, when I’m not buried in schoolwork or trying to look like I have my life together, I’m probably talking about the things I love. A lot. I’m a very, very proud yapper; once I start rambling about my favorite things, there’s no stopping me. I am a comic reader, but I mostly read DC comics. My favourite characters so far are Bart Allen (Impulse), Courtney Whitmore (Stargirl), Stephanie Brown (Spoiler), and Damian Wayne (Robin), and I would list them all, but I fear it would take hours of your time. I could literally talk about my favourite characters, especially Bart, all day. Besides characters, I’ve been reading a lot of JSA, Young Justice, Wonder Woman, and so much more. My favourite team is probably both the JSA and Young Justice, but with the JSA taking the lead. Other than comics, I read a lot of books. I mostly read classic books, action, mystery, or thriller. I’m not that much of a fan of romance books, honestly, considering I read a lot of action-themed comics. I also like playing video games like Minecraft, Roblox, or Call of Duty. While I may not be good at PVP, you cannot say that I don’t play Minecraft well. Anyway, I’ve also been enjoying thriller or action movies, and I’m currently watching a TV series called Stargirl (2020). Whether it’s comics, movies, or random shows, I’ll find a way to bring it up in conversation. It’s one of my favorite things, really, talking about the stories and heroes that make me feel a little more human.
